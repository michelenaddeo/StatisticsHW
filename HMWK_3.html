  • Rademacher: Xi = ±1 with probability ½ (a fair balance of successes and failures). μ = 0, σ² = 1.
  • The m attackers are m independent copies of the process; E[Sn] = nμ and Var(Sn) = nσ².

5Take-aways

Law of large numbers. The average Sn/n funnels to μ: the probability of missing μ by more than ε goes to 0 (Chebyshev: ≤ σ²/(nε²)).
Variances. nσ² grows, σ²/n shrinks, σ² stays constant: only the √n scaling gives a stable limit.
Central limit theorem. Sn/√n → N(0,1) in distribution (Rademacher; Bernoulli after centring), although each single path keeps wandering. With discrete steps Dn cannot drop below the lattice jump (~1/√n).
Systems and attackers. More systems: the penetration rate becomes predictable. More attackers: a clearer distribution and a more extreme best attacker, about nμ + σ√(2n ln m).
